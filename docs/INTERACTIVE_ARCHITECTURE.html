End-to-end flow

  • Patient records movement and describes symptoms.
  • Local models produce measurements and safety flags.
  • Therapist reviews evidence and approves a decision.
  • Local AI drafts a tutorial from verified facts.
  • FFmpeg/libass adds captions; Piper or espeak adds optional voice.
  • Patient sees only approved tutorials and media.
